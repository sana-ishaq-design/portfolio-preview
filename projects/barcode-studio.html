Barcode Studio

Project note · Chrome extension · Product design

Barcode Studio lets users generate QR codes and linear barcodes — EAN-13, Code 128, UPC-A — directly inside a Chrome popup and export them as PNG, SVG, WebP, or PDF, individually or in bulk.

Role
End-to-End UX · Design + Handoff

Design focus

No account. No server. No tab switch required.

The product serves a precise moment: a user is mid-task, needs a barcode in seconds, and does not want the overhead of a new tab, an account, or an external upload.
